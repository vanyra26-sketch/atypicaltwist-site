💡 A Note on Pricing

Some members may have seen lower fares advertised through Carnival's targeted offers or Fun Saver specials. Those rates apply to the lowest available cabin categories, which typically come with less desirable ship locations and potentially obstructed views. The categories secured for the Patriots group were intentionally selected to ensure a comfortable, well-located experience on the ship. If you have questions about specific pricing or cabin options, please reach out directly — I'm happy to walk you through everything.

Required for All Travelers
ID Verification
🔒 Carnival requires a valid government-issued ID on file for every traveler before a stateroom can be secured. Once you submit the booking form and provide your email address, I will send you a secure upload link directly. Please have your ID ready by April 11, 2026.

🇺🇸 U.S. Citizens

Passport book (strongly recommended), passport card, REAL ID driver's license + birth certificate, or Enhanced Driver's License.

🟢 Resident Aliens

Valid unexpired Alien Resident Card (Form I-551 / Green Card). Home country passport also recommended.

🌍 Non-U.S. Citizens

Valid passport from country of citizenship, valid for at least 6 months beyond travel completion. Applicable visas required.

Got Questions?
Frequently Asked
What's included in my cruise fare? +
Your cruise fare includes accommodations, meals in the main dining room and buffet, most onboard entertainment, access to pools and amenities, and your $50 onboard credit. Specialty dining, shore excursions, drinks, spa, and gratuities are additional.
How do I get pricing information? +
Pricing is shared privately per Carnival's group booking guidelines. Complete the booking form or contact Vanessa directly at 954-546-1546 and she'll provide your personalized rate based on cabin type, occupancy, and any special requirements.
What documents do I need to board? +
Since this is a closed-loop sailing (Miami to Miami), U.S. citizens may travel with a valid government-issued photo ID plus an original birth certificate. A valid passport is strongly recommended. Non-U.S. citizens must have a valid passport.
Can I add a 3rd or 4th guest to my cabin? +
Yes — 3rd and 4th guests in the same cabin typically pay a reduced rate. Use the Notes field in the booking form to ask about multi-guest pricing and Vanessa will provide a custom quote for your specific cabin type.
What happens if I need to cancel? +
Cancellation penalties apply based on how close to sailing you cancel. Deposits are forfeited through May 12. After May 13 a 50% penalty applies, increasing to 75% after June 2, and 100% after July 13. Travel protection is strongly recommended.
Is travel insurance available? +
Yes — Carnival's Vacation Protection Plan is available and strongly recommended. Premiums vary by cabin type and must be paid by the final payment date. Ask Vanessa for details when you book.
I'm traveling solo — can I still join the group? +
Absolutely! Single occupancy is available at 200% of the per-person double rate. Contact Vanessa directly for a single occupancy quote and she'll find the best option for you.
